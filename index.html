How it works

  • 1Paste your material. Notes, slides, an article or a glossary — the generator pulls out the key terms and ideas.
  • 2Set the deadline and mark. Lessons are spread across the days you have, with more per day for higher targets.
  • 3Follow the path. Each lesson is a short round of questions. Finish one to unlock the next — collect XP and keep your streak.
  • 4Lose a heart for a wrong answer. Hearts refill over time, so a mistake is never the end of the road.

Everything is stored locally in your browser. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Duolingo.
